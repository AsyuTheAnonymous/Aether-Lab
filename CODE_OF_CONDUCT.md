# Code of Conduct

Aether Lab is a small project made by people who like fixing old games. We want it to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers. Everyone asks "why won't my patch apply?" at some point.
- Assume good intent, and critique ideas and code, not people.
- Give credit to the romhackers, tool authors and homebrew developers whose work this builds on.
- Accept a maintainer's decision gracefully, even when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual content or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Posting or requesting ROMs, keys, firmware or links to them. This is a legal risk for everyone involved.
- Spam and deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Enforcement

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through [silvea.tech](https://silvea.tech). Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is.
